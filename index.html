4. Cookie and Storage Framework

We do not run tracking cookies, profiling pixels, or marketing scripts. We may periodically use local browser cache states solely to preserve your visual configuration preferences (such as directory routing memory) to expedite load times on subsequent visits.

5. Global Policy Conformity

Because Covvy's World does not capture, store, or sell any personal data pipelines, our platform remains structurally compliant with international data safety benchmarks, including the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA).
